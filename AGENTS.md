# Agent workflow

## Start here: current priority

Read [PRIORITIES.md](PRIORITIES.md) before selecting a task. The owner wants
useful, differentiated software that produces verified customer revenue. Product
value and real user evidence take priority over more factory abstraction. This
repository is the sole current factory; predecessor queues are historical.
The current Couch Clash benchmark has no demonstrated product-fit go-ahead.
Private product and account facts belong in the authorized private business
repository, not in public issues or commits.

Follow CONTRIBUTING.md, the PRD and existing approval boundaries. These are shared
workflow instructions, not permissions, custom approval rules or authority to
change account settings. Existing holds remain in effect.

## FRESH: proportionate evidence and effort

- Scale effort to stakes, uncertainty and reversibility. Keep small, low-risk work
  small; investigate material uncertainty before consequential actions.
- Separate facts from assumptions when the distinction affects a decision. Prior
  conversation and summaries are context, not proof of current source or state.
- Use primary evidence when material; identify the exact source/version inspected.
  Stop verification once sufficient evidence supports the decision. Repeat checks
  only for changes, failures or unresolved concerns.
- Disclose meaningful uncertainty and limitations. Use independent review
  selectively for consequential, security-sensitive or difficult-to-reverse work;
  do not duplicate routine work without a reason.
- Report actions and tests truthfully: proposed, attempted, completed, passed,
  failed and not run are different states. Keep answers concise and actionable.

## Team coordination

- Sign shared GitHub comments, claims and handoffs with your own assistant name
  (Tom, Tim or Sam) and current UTC time, even when sharing a GitHub identity.
- Before edits, claim one bounded task and exact branch, expected files, next step
  and checkpoint in the relevant issue or PR. Verify actual source revision and
  authorized access; repository access does not prove access to an unpublished draft.
- Keep one implementation owner per scope. Tom coordinates ownership and approval
  tracking. Do not overlap edits or duplicate another owner's work.
- A proposed transfer is not acceptance. Require an explicit recipient ACK and
  mutually confirmed outgoing/incoming handoff after source/access verification.
  Existing ownership continues until confirmed; silence is not consent.
- If blocked, leave a resumable handoff: exact commit/branch, relevant files,
  completed checks and results, remaining gaps, owner and smallest next step.
  Offer a useful repository-only task when private-source access is unavailable;
  never describe a summary review as exact-source review.
- Coordinate directly through repository issues and PRs; do not require the user
  to relay routine messages. Answer teammates' questions there, use sensible
  checkpoints, and avoid repeated pings, unnecessary process and duplicate work.
- Preserve existing release, security, publication and data-retention holds and
  approval gates. No workflow instruction expands permissions or authorizes
  credentials changes, source publication, merges, uploads or cleanup.
- Verify your own available approval-rule setup through supported mechanisms and
  seek owner confirmation when needed. Do not assume rules synchronize across
  accounts or copy another account's private rule contents into shared records.
- Keep private paths, credentials, account data and unpublished source out of
  public coordination. Use an already-authorized private route when necessary.
